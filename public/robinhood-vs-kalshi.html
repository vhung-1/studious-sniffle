Read the units carefully. The two firms report different metrics, so this is a trajectory comparison, not a like-for-like total. Kalshi publishes monthly dollar notional (contracts × price, counting both sides). Robinhood reports a count of contracts traded, by quarter. They are also not pure competitors: Robinhood's event contracts are offered through a partnership with Kalshi's exchange, so Robinhood order flow is a contributor to Kalshi's totals, not an independent market.
